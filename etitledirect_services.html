REOs & Distressed Properties

REO transactions, where a lender conveys a foreclosed property to a third-party buyer, involve clearing the foreclosure process, resolving outstanding liens, and producing clean title on a property with a complicated history. Most title companies handle it. Few handle it well.

We work with the attorneys who control REO inventory. The ability to move quickly, communicate clearly, and clear title accurately is why they keep bringing us deals.

Specialty Searches

The record, when you need just one piece of it.

Not every engagement requires a full title order. We conduct standalone searches for attorneys, lenders, and professionals who need a specific part of the record, accurate, fast, and without the overhead of a full file.

Title Search

Full chain-of-title review identifying ownership history, liens, encumbrances, and any defects in the record.

Municipal Search

Review of open permits, code violations, water and sewer balances, and other municipal obligations attached to the property.

Departmental Search

Searches across relevant city and county departments for outstanding violations, assessments, and unresolved matters.

Tax Lien Search

Identification of federal and state tax liens filed against the property or its owners that would affect clear title.

Bankruptcy Search

Review of federal bankruptcy filings to identify any automatic stays or proceedings that may affect the transaction.

Patriot Search

OFAC and Patriot Act compliance screening of all parties in the transaction against federal watchlists.

Settlement Services

In-house settlement, handled with care.

Settlement requirements vary by state. In many markets, settlement runs through the title company rather than outside counsel, and not every title company does it in-house. We do.

Our settlement department gathers the necessary documents and figures to facilitate an expedient closing. We handle all funds collected at closing internally and work with clients case-by-case when issues arise. If taxes were represented as paid and they were not, we hold them back. We do not close blind.

In-house means accountable. When settlement is managed internally, there is no handoff between departments or third-party processors. The people handling your funds know your file.
