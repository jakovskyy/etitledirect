Co-Founder

David Fernandez

Co-Founder, eTitleDirect

With over twenty years in real estate, David originally began in construction before he ever worked in title. Starting as a foreman, he progressed to superintendent, then project manager, and eventually became the owner's representative for a major Brooklyn developer, where he handled land acquisitions, financing, development planning, and marketing across multiple projects throughout New York City.

From there he moved into residential sales, becoming the top producer at his firm within two years. David was able to quickly grow from a successful independent agent to a reputable team lead on multiple new developments throughout NYC. A firm believer in progressive expansion, he has always held that the quality of a team's brand and the level of service it can offer should be ever-improving. With his collective industry knowledge and customized approach, David and his team have continuously reinvented themselves to tailor their abilities to each client's unique needs.

That foundation is what led him to co-found eTitleDirect. He built it to fix the parts of the closing process that consistently failed everyone else at the table.
