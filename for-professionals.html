For Lenders

Clean commitments. No boilerplate exceptions.

Most title companies pad their commitments with generic, catch-all language that has no bearing on the property in front of you. We don't. Every exception in our commitment is there because it pertains to that specific parcel. If it doesn't apply, it's not in there. Your underwriting team will notice.

Exception discipline. No "easements not of record" language on a property with no easement history. We insure what you expect to be insured.

Lenders' counsel coordination. In commercial deals, title companies that don't communicate with lenders' counsel are the last to get deals. We work directly with your counsel on clearance and coordination.

CPL issuance and gap coverage. Issued on your schedule. Funding coordination is structured, documented, and communicated without you having to chase it down.
