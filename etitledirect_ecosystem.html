Where buyers and sellers
find their way home.

The real estate transaction is the most complicated financial event in most people's lives, and most buyers have no idea what's happening between contract and closing. ClosingMap changes that. It gives consumers a clear, real-time view of their transaction, connects them with the professionals working their file, and keeps everyone informed at every stage.

Real-time transaction milestone tracking from contract to closing
Document access and status updates in one organized place
Direct connection to the agent, lender, and title team on the file
Closing cost visibility and timeline at every step

CloseProp

The deal room for real
estate professionals.

Agents, lenders, and attorneys all working on the same transaction rarely have a shared view of it. CloseProp gives every professional on a deal one place to track progress, exchange documents, manage deadlines, and communicate, so the transaction stays moving and no one is left out of the loop.

Shared deal dashboard for all parties on a transaction
Deadline tracking and automated milestone alerts
Document management with role-based access for every party
CRM layer connecting agents with their current and past clients

TytlLab

TytlLab is the AI-powered title operating system being built to run beneath every eTitleDirect transaction. It replicates repeatable operational workflows, reduces manual workload across every department, and increases the transaction intelligence available at every stage of the process, from order to final recording.

Automated workflow orchestration from order form to post-close recording
Intelligent document analysis and exception identification
Scalable infrastructure that grows with transaction volume
Cross-department transaction intelligence and operational analytics

The Full Picture

Three products. One connected close.

Every real estate transaction involves a buyer, a set of professionals, and a title company. Each product in the ecosystem is built specifically for one of those layers, and they are designed to work in concert, so that information flows cleanly and every party has exactly what they need to move forward.

ClosingMap
For Consumers

Buyers and sellers get real-time visibility into the transaction they are part of, without having to call anyone to find out what is happening.

CloseProp
For Professionals

Agents, lenders, and attorneys work the deal from one shared platform, so deadlines get hit, documents get exchanged, and no one is operating on outdated information.

TytlLab
Intelligence Layer

The title operation runs on intelligent, automated infrastructure from the moment an order is placed through the final recording, reducing manual work and increasing accuracy at every step.
